# Policy Authority Register (Current)

**AUTH-2026-P1** For the same topic and date, an applicable current policy outranks an archived policy.

**AUTH-2026-P2** A current, explicit department exception outranks the general policy for that department.

**AUTH-2026-P3** An FAQ explains policy but does not override it.

**AUTH-2026-P4** If two applicable authoritative documents truly conflict, flag the conflict rather than guess.
